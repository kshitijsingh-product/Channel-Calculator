Assumptions

Answered calls ring for 15 seconds before pickup. Unanswered attempts hold a channel for 30 seconds. Call setup time is ignored. The buffer covers normal swings in call volume about 98% of the time. Telco CPS is taken as 4% of your channels, the low end of the usual 4–8% range.
